What we stand for

People decisions, handled properly

Flaming Oak Consulting Ltd is an HR services and business consulting firm working with organisations in Nairobi and beyond. We step in wherever a business needs structured, dependable people expertise — sourcing the right candidates, building the policies that keep a company compliant and fair, training teams, and, where needed, managing a department directly.

Our work sits between HR administration and business strategy: we're as comfortable running an assessment centre for shortlisted candidates as we are speaking to a room of managers about performance culture, or rewriting a policy manual so it actually gets used.
